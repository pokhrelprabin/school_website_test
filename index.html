Legacy

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Established in 2055 B.S., Dharan Higher Secondary School has been a cornerstone of quality education in Sunsari district. Located at Laxmi Sadak, Dharan-1, we are committed to nurturing bright young minds through rigorous academics and holistic development.

Our dedicated faculty and modern infrastructure create an environment where students excel not only in board examinations but also in competitive entrance tests for medical, engineering, and management fields.
